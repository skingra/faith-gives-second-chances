Who we are

A house started by people who've walked this road

Faith Gives Second Chances began with a simple conviction: recovery holds when it's built on faith, real structure, and people who won't give up on you. What started as a single home for a handful of residents has grown into a full program run by staff, mentors, and volunteers — many of whom carry their own story of restoration.

We work hand in hand with local churches, families, and treatment partners, because no one rebuilds a life alone.

Our mission

We believe recovery is not just abstinence — it is restoration of identity, purpose, and family.

Every resident enters with a different story and leaves with a shared foundation: sobriety anchored in faith, character formed through daily structure, and relationships rebuilt on trust. We work alongside local churches and families, not apart from them.
Faith

We anchor every part of the program in scripture and prayer, not just program milestones.

Accountability

Structure and honesty aren't punishment — they're how trust gets rebuilt, one day at a time.

Restoration

We treat the whole person: identity, purpose, and relationships, not just sobriety alone.

Community

No one graduates this program alone — staff, mentors, churches, and family walk it together.

Journey of hope

Three phases, one path forward

The program is sequential by design — each phase builds the stability the next one requires, from first steps to independent living.

01
FoundationMonths 1–6
Residents settle into structure — daily accountability, sobriety fundamentals, house responsibilities, and consistent mentorship from staff and peers who've walked the same road.
02
FormationMonths 7–14
Character and identity work deepens through our curricula tracks, church involvement, and mentorship — while residents begin re-engaging with work and family relationships.
03
FruitfulnessMonths 15–18
Residents prepare to transition into independent housing, steady employment, and restored family roles — with an ongoing alumni and church support network.

Programs & services

Four curriculum tracks, one purpose

Alongside recovery work, residents study one of four core curricula built around the roles they're rebuilding their lives to fill.

Manhood

Character, responsibility, and leadership formation for men re-establishing their footing.

Motherhood

Restoring confidence and practical skill for women returning to the role of mother.

Marriage

Rebuilding trust and communication for residents working toward or within marriage.

Family

Reconnecting residents with children, parents, and extended family after seasons apart.

Who we serve

Built for people ready to do the work

We serve men and women coming out of addiction, incarceration, or crisis who are committed to a structured, faith-centered path to independence.

Coming out of treatment

Residents transitioning from detox or inpatient treatment who need sober, structured housing to keep their footing.

Reentering after incarceration

Men and women rebuilding a stable life and record after release, with accountability and community around them.

Ready to reconnect with family

Residents working to restore relationships with children, spouses, and parents on a realistic timeline.
